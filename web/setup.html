SET UP & CHECK

Make DashBridge yours.

Connect a board with a USB data cable. You can connect both boards to check the full path. Your choices are saved on the iPhone board.

Connect your boards

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Close any other USB log window first.

Checking browser…

No boards connected yet. Connect Board A to choose apps, or Board B to check the Tesla side.

Connection check

These checks update while the boards are connected. Being away from the car is normal.

A Bluetooth connection cannot confirm that a message appeared on the Tesla screen or that both sides of a call can hear each other.

Apps allowed on your Tesla

Only apps you allow can send notification text through DashBridge.

Connect Board A to choose apps.

To find an app, select “Find an app,” then make that app send a new iPhone notification within one minute. DashBridge learns its name without reading the notification text. The app must have iPhone notifications enabled.

Try it

Open pairing for the board you are setting up, or send a test message from Board B when Tesla message sync is ready.

Settings stay on your board. No account or server is used for configuration.
