# DashBridge 0.4.0-alpha

This prerelease brings the two-board prototype beyond notifications and basic
calls. It is **not a verified daily-use adapter**. Both firmware images build
and pass host checks, but these exact release images have not been installed and
tested with an iPhone and Tesla. Use the [README](../../README.md#project-status)
for the current feature-by-feature evidence and parked-car checklist.

## Added since 0.3.3-alpha

- Recent WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business notifications that are still available
  through iOS ANCS can be imported silently into the Tesla's read-only inbox
  when the bridge reconnects. This is not WhatsApp chat history or an offline
  archive.
- HFP control now includes call-list forwarding, last-number redial, and
  call-waiting/hold/conference commands when the iPhone and Tesla expose them.
- The call path relays encoded mSBC (16 kHz HD when negotiated) or CVSD (8 kHz)
  audio between the boards. This replaces the earlier PCM relay. The old
  tone/loopback isolation commands are unavailable in this encoded build.
- A2DP SBC music relay and AVRCP play/pause, skip, seek, and track/playback
  metadata are implemented. Music is audio and basic controls only: no album
  artwork, library browsing, search, queue, shuffle, or repeat.
- PBAP client/server paths were added for contacts, favorites, and
  incoming/outgoing/missed/combined call lists. vCards keep multiple labelled
  numbers and addresses grouped under a contact.
- The active release contains only the matching Board A and Board B images.
  The obsolete `single-merged.bin` and standalone HFP reconnect reproduction
  were removed from the active tree; their history remains in Git. The original
  single-board prototype remains available from the `archive/single-board`
  branch.

## What is verified, and what is not

- Both ESP32 images build with pinned ESP-IDF v5.5.5. Notification, contact,
  PBAP, call-control, audio-transport, and music host tests pass.
- The immediately preceding development images were flashed and booted. Board A
  reached an encrypted ANCS connection and linked iPhone A2DP/AVRCP, with
  metadata callbacks. Earlier two-board builds showed real WhatsApp text on
  the Tesla and negotiated mSBC on both call links.
- In the latest development hardware log, **all six iPhone phonebook/history
  pulls failed** (`entries=0`, `bytes=0`, `discarded=6`). Contact names,
  favorites, and call history are therefore **not demonstrated** on the Tesla.
- No actual music stream or Tesla media-button press was confirmed. Call audio
  previously sounded robotic or unclear, and the new encoded path has **not
  had a listening test**. Conference, redial, recent-message import, and
  sustained reconnection also need real-device checks.
- WhatsApp replies/sending, Siri, and voice-assistant integration are not
  implemented. Bluetooth links and passing software tests alone do not prove
  these services work with a particular iPhone or Tesla.

Do not remove a working direct phone option until clear bidirectional audio,
reconnection, and phone-key coexistence have been tested while parked.
